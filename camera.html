Enhanced Camera System

A data-driven camera modifier pipeline for Unreal Engine 5.3. Layer camera behaviors, procedural FOV zoom transitions, occlusion lag dampening, and procedural shakes via modular modifiers without hard actor references.

01 / ARCHITECTURE
  • Modifier Stack Architecture: Dynamically push, pop, or blend camera modifiers (e.g., sprint shake, aiming zoom, cinematic lock).
  • Data Asset Profiles: Define camera behaviors entirely in Data Assets and swap profiles at runtime with a single node.
  • Sequencer Interoperability: Seamlessly blends between gameplay cameras and cinematic camera actors without frame popping.
